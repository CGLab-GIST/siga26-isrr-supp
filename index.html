Image-Space Russian Roulette for Monte Carlo Rendering

Geonu Noh, Chanu Yang , Bochang Moon
Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)

We compare our method with baseline path tracing, practical path guiding (PPG) [Müller et al. 2017], and EARS, a recent vertex-level Russian roulette method [Rath et al. 2022], under equal-time settings. Please click any image below to inspect the corresponding results in detail.
